From level 1 to 5

Shoes off. Feel the moss. You've grown four seasons in a single breath, so let's walk through what the forest has given you, one step at a time.

Arrive

A few choices to make

No rush. Sit with each one. The rest of this page will help.

  • Choose your groveYour circle: Land, Moon, Sea, or Stars. They're compared at the bottom.
  • Choose a featAt level 4: Ability Score Improvement (raise your scores) or another feat you qualify for.
  • Learn one cantripThat makes 3. If you took Magician at level 1, it's 4.
  • Gather 5 more spellsYou go from 4 prepared spells to 9. Pick from the lists below.
  • Befriend 6 animalsThe beasts you can become. Up to about as tough as a warhorse, nothing with wings yet.
  • Grow your hit pointsLook up your Constitution below; no math needed.
  • Ask your DM about gearNew level 5 characters often get extra gold and a couple of magic items. Their call.
Breathe

Your hit points

Breathe in. Four new levels settle into your bones.

Wander

What each step of the path gave you

Walk it slowly, one level at a time.

Level 2

Wild Shape p. 80

Spend a bonus action to become one of the animals you know. You keep your mind and your own hit points, and get a cushion of extra temporary hit points equal to your level (5 now). You can't cast spells while shifted. It lasts up to 2 hours at your level, or until you choose to come back.

Level 2

Wild Companion p. 81

Spend a spell slot or a Wild Shape use and a little fey spirit joins you as a familiar (Find Familiar, no materials needed). It slips away when you next take a long rest.

Level 3

Your circle p. 81

You join a grove. You get its level 3 gifts now, and its level 5 spells too. See below.

Level 4

A feat, a cantrip, more animals pp. 79–81

Pick your feat. Most druids raise Wisdom, since it powers your spells. You learn a third cantrip, and you can now know 6 animals, up to about as tough as a warhorse (challenge rating ½).

Level 5

Wild Resurgence p. 81

Magic and wildness can trade places. Out of Wild Shape? Spend a spell slot to get a use back. Short on spells? Once a day, spend a Wild Shape use to get a level 1 slot.

Choose a grove

Four circles, four ways to belong

Stand in each one for a moment. Which one feels like home?

"The land speaks differently everywhere I walk. I listen, and borrow its voice."

Circle of the Land

Land's Aid. Spend a Wild Shape use to make a burst of flowers and thorns somewhere nearby: enemies inside take 2d6 necrotic damage (half if they pass a Constitution save), and one friend inside heals 2d6. pp. 84–85

Borrowed spells. Each morning, pick a kind of land. Its spells are prepared for free:

Circle of the Moon

Stronger shapes. Your animal forms can be as tough as a brown bear (challenge rating 1). In animal form your Armor Class is at least 13 plus your Wisdom modifier, and your cushion of temporary hit points is 15 instead of 5. p. 86

Moon spells, prepared for free, and you can cast them even in animal form:

3Cure Wounds3Moonbeam3Starry Wisp5Conjure Animals

"I keep a storm in my pocket and salt in my hair."

Circle of the Sea

Wrath of the Sea. Spend a Wild Shape use and sea spray swirls around you for 10 minutes. Each turn, as a bonus action, you can lash one creature beside you with cold and shove it back 15 feet, unless it shrugs it off with a Constitution save. Damage: roll one d6 for each point of your Wisdom modifier, so +3 Wisdom means 3d6. p. 87

Sea spells, prepared for free:

3Fog Cloud3Gust of Wind3Ray of Frost3Shatter3Thunderwave5Lightning Bolt5Water Breathing

"When the canopy parts, I read the sky like a letter from an old friend."

Circle of the Stars

Star Map. A little star chart you carry. While you hold it you always have Guidance and Guiding Bolt, and you can cast Guiding Bolt for free a few times a day: as many times as your Wisdom modifier. pp. 88–89

Starry Form. Spend a Wild Shape use to glow with constellations for 10 minutes instead of becoming an animal. Pick one each time:

Archershoot a starlight arrow each turnChaliceyour healing spells heal a little extraDragonlow rolls count as 10 when thinking or holding focus

Rest here a while. The path keeps going: a gift from your circle at level 6, Elemental Fury at 7, and wings for your animal forms at 8.
