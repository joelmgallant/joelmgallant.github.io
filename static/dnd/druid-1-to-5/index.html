Druid, level 1 to 5

Everything a level 1 druid picks up when she jumps straight to level 5: the choices to make, the new numbers, each feature in the order the levels grant it, and what each circle offers.

Choices to make

PHB pp. 42, 79–89
  • Pick a circleLand, Moon, Sea, or Stars (level 3). Compared below.
  • Pick a featAbility Score Improvement, or any feat she qualifies for (level 4).
  • Learn 1 cantripTakes her to 3 cantrips, or 4 with the Magician primal order (level 4).
  • Prepare 5 more spellsFrom 4 to 9 prepared, any mix of level 1 to 3 druid spells.
  • Learn 6 beast formsBeasts of CR ½ or lower with no fly speed. Swap one per long rest.
  • Add hit pointsFor each of levels 2 to 5: 5 + Con modifier, or roll 1d8 + Con.
  • Ask the DM about gearStarting fresh at level 5 suggests 500 GP + 1d10 × 25 GP, one common and one uncommon magic item (p. 43).

New features, level by level

PHB pp. 80–81
2Level

Wild Shapep. 80

Bonus action to turn into a known beast form. She keeps her mind, hit points and class features, gains temporary HP equal to her druid level (5 now), and can't cast spells while shifted. Lasts up to half her druid level in hours. One use back on a short rest, all back on a long rest.

Wild Companionp. 81

Spend a spell slot or a Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar with no material cost. The familiar is a fey spirit and leaves at her next long rest.

3Level

Druid circlep. 81

Her subclass. She gets its level 3 features right away, plus its level 5 spells where it has them. See the circles below.

4Level

Featp. 81

Ability Score Improvement (two +1s or one +2), or another feat. Wisdom drives her spells and her circle features.

Third cantrip, sixth beast formpp. 79, 81

Beast forms can now be CR ½ (up from ¼). Still no flying forms until level 8.

5Level

Wild Resurgencep. 81

When she's out of Wild Shape uses, she can spend a spell slot to get one back (once per turn). She can also spend a Wild Shape use for a level 1 spell slot (once per long rest).

Pick a circle

PHB pp. 84–89 · everything through level 5
Flexible caster

Circle of the Land

PHB pp. 84–85

Land's Aid. Spend a Wild Shape use as a magic action: a 10 ft sphere within 60 ft. Enemies make a Con save or take 2d6 necrotic (half on success), and one creature in it heals 2d6.

Circle spells, always prepared. She picks a land type at each long rest:

Front-line shapeshifter

Circle of the Moon

PHB p. 86

Circle Forms. Her beast forms can be up to CR 1 (druid level ÷ 3), her AC becomes 13 + Wis modifier if that beats the beast's, and she gains 15 temporary HP (3 × druid level) instead of 5.

Circle spells, always prepared, and castable while in Wild Shape:

Storm and control

Circle of the Sea

PHB p. 87

Wrath of the Sea. Spend a Wild Shape use as a bonus action for a 5 ft aura of ocean spray lasting 10 minutes. As a bonus action, one creature in it makes a Con save or takes cold damage (one d6 per point of Wis modifier) and is pushed 15 ft if Large or smaller.

Circle spells, always prepared:

3Fog Cloud3Gust of Wind3Ray of Frost3Shatter3Thunderwave5Lightning Bolt5Water Breathing

Circle of the Stars

PHB pp. 88–89

Star Map. A Tiny star chart that works as her focus. While holding it she has Guidance and Guiding Bolt prepared, and can cast Guiding Bolt without a slot a number of times equal to her Wis modifier per long rest.

Starry Form. Spend a Wild Shape use as a bonus action to glow for 10 minutes instead of shifting, keeping her own stats. She picks a constellation each time:
