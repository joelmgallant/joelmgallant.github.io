Sorcerer PHB p. 139

A sorcerer knows fewer spells but can mess with how they work. Want your spell cast silently, or a bit faster, or aimed at two people at once? That's what sorcery points are for.

  • Innate Sorcery (level 1, p. 140). For a minute your spells are harder to resist and easier to hit with. Twice a day.
  • Sorcery points and Metamagic (level 2, pp. 140–141). You have 5 points and know 2 ways to change a spell, like Subtle, Quickened or Twinned. You can also melt a spare slot into points, or turn 5 points into an extra level 3 slot.
  • Sorcerous Restoration (level 5, p. 141). A short rest gives you 2 points back, once a day.

Origins, chosen at level 3

Aberrant p. 145. Something alien got into your head. You can talk to someone mind to mind, even from miles away.

Free spells: Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver, and at 5, Hunger of Hadar and Sending.

Clockwork p. 146. Your power comes from cosmic order. A few times a day you can cancel someone's advantage or disadvantage so they roll straight.

Free spells: Aid, Alarm, Lesser Restoration, Protection from Evil and Good, and at 5, Dispel Magic and Protection from Energy.

Draconic p. 148. There's a dragon in the family. You get extra hit points (+5 at level 5) and decent natural armor.

Free spells: Alter Self, Chromatic Orb, Command, Dragon's Breath, and at 5, Fear and Fly.

Wild Magic p. 149. Your spells sometimes set off random surges, and now and then you can give yourself advantage on a roll. There's no free spell list with this one.

Warlock PHB p. 153

A warlock only gets two spell slots, but they're always at full strength and they come back after a short rest. If your group takes a lot of breathers, that adds up fast. Warlocks are also a little tougher, with more hit points and light armor.

  • Eldritch Invocations (level 1, p. 153). Permanent magic secrets. You have 5 by level 5. The pacts people talk about (a summoned weapon, a familiar, a book of extra magic) are picked from these.
  • Pact Magic (level 1, pp. 153–154). Your two slots are level 3 now and refill on a short rest.
  • Magical Cunning (level 2, p. 154). A minute-long ritual that gets one slot back, once a day.

Patrons, chosen at level 3

Archfey p. 159. A fey noble. You can blink a short distance for free a few times a day, and each blink comes with a bonus, like a few temporary hit points.

Free spells: Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Sleep, and at 5, Blink and Plant Growth.

Celestial p. 160. A heavenly patron. You carry a pool of healing dice (6d6 at level 5) to hand out as a bonus action.

Free spells: Aid, Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, Lesser Restoration, Light, Sacred Flame, and at 5, Daylight and Revivify.

Fiend p. 161. A demon or devil. Every time an enemy drops near you, you get temporary hit points. Lots of fire.

Free spells: Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray, Suggestion, and at 5, Fireball and Stinking Cloud.

Great Old One pp. 162–163. Something huge and ancient. Mind-to-mind talking at long range, psychic damage on demand, and you can cast charms and illusions without a word or a gesture.

Free spells: Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Phantasmal Force, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, and at 5, Clairvoyance and Hunger of Hadar.

Wizard PHB p. 165

The wizard has the most spells by far, all written in a book, and gets to change which ones are ready every morning. Good if you like planning ahead and collecting things.

  • The spellbook (level 1, pp. 165–167). It has 14 spells by level 5, and you can copy in spells you find on adventures (2 hours and 50 gp per spell level).
  • Ritual Adept (level 1, p. 166). Ritual spells can be cast straight from the book without preparing them.
  • Arcane Recovery (level 1, p. 166). Once a day on a short rest, get back one level 3 slot, or a level 2 and a level 1.
  • Scholar (level 2, p. 166). Expertise in one bookish skill, like Arcana or History.
  • Memorize Spell (level 5, p. 167). On a short rest, swap one ready spell for another from the book.

Schools, chosen at level 3

Schools don't come with free spell lists. Each one adds 2 free spells of its school to your book when you pick it, and one more each time a new spell level opens up.

Abjurer p. 172. A magic ward that takes hits for you. Casting protective spells tops it back up.

Diviner p. 173. Every morning you roll two d20s and keep the results. Later you can swap one in for any roll you can see, yours or anyone else's.

Evoker p. 174. Your damage cantrips still do half damage when they miss.

Illusionist p. 175. Silent illusions with extra range, and a free Minor Illusion you can cast as a bonus action.

Can't decide? If you want to show off with a few spells, sorcerer. If you want toughness and a patron with a story, warlock. If you want every spell in the world, wizard. And tell your DM everything. The magician insists.
