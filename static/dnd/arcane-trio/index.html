Sorcerer PHB p. 139

A sorcerer knows fewer spells but can mess with how they work. Want your spell cast silently, or a bit faster, or aimed at two people at once? That's what sorcery points are for.

  • Innate Sorcery (level 1, p. 140). For a minute your spells are harder to resist and easier to hit with. Twice a day.
  • Sorcery points and Metamagic (level 2, pp. 140–141). You have 5 points and know 2 ways to change a spell, like Subtle, Quickened or Twinned. You can also melt a spare slot into points, or turn 5 points into an extra level 3 slot.
  • Sorcerous Restoration (level 5, p. 141). A short rest gives you 2 points back, once a day.

Origins, chosen at level 3

Aberrant p. 145. Something alien got into your head. You can talk to someone mind to mind, even from miles away.

Free spells:

  • Arms of HadarTendrils burst out in a 10-foot Emanation. Creatures make a Strength save or take 2d6 Necrotic damage and can't take Reactions; half damage on a save.
  • Calm EmotionsHumanoids in a 20-foot radius make Charisma saves. Affected ones either become immune to Charmed and Frightened or turn Indifferent to chosen foes.
  • Detect ThoughtsSense nearby minds within 30 feet, or read the surface thoughts of a creature you can see. Deeper probing makes the target roll a Wisdom save.
  • Dissonant WhispersOne creature within range hears a hideous melody: Wisdom save or take 3d6 Psychic damage and use its Reaction to run away from you. Half damage on a save.
  • Mind SliverA creature within 60 feet makes an Intelligence save or takes 1d6 Psychic damage and subtracts 1d4 from its next saving throw before your next turn ends. Grows at levels 5, 11 and 17.
  • Hunger of Hadarat 5Open a 20-foot-radius sphere of Darkness that's Difficult Terrain and blinds anyone inside. Creatures take 2d6 Cold damage starting their turn there, or 2d6 Acid (Dexterity save) ending it there.
  • Sendingat 5Send a message of 25 words or fewer to a creature you know of, at any distance. They hear it in their mind and can reply right away, though the message may fail across planes.

Clockwork p. 146. Your power comes from cosmic order. A few times a day you can cancel someone's advantage or disadvantage so they roll straight.

Free spells:

  • AidUp to three creatures within 30 feet gain 5 extra Hit Points, both current and maximum, for 8 hours. Slots above 2nd add 5 more per level.
  • AlarmWard a door, window or 20-foot area for 8 hours. You get a loud bell sound nearby or a silent mental ping if anything touches or enters it.
  • Lesser RestorationTouch a creature and, as a Bonus Action, end one condition on it: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned.
  • Protection from Evil and GoodA touched ally is warded against Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends and Undead. They attack at Disadvantage, and can't possess or Charm or Frighten the ward.
  • Dispel Magicat 5End a spell on a creature, object or effect in range. Level 3 or lower ends automatically; higher ones need a spellcasting check against DC 10 plus the spell level.
  • Protection from Energyat 5A willing creature you touch gets Resistance to one damage type you pick: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. Lasts up to an hour.

Draconic p. 148. There's a dragon in the family. You get extra hit points (+5 at level 5) and decent natural armor.

Free spells:

  • Alter SelfReshape your body: grow gills and a Swim Speed, change your looks to anyone of your size and shape, or grow claws, fangs or horns for 1d6 unarmed strikes.
  • Chromatic OrbHurl an orb of Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison or Thunder at a target within 90 feet: 3d8 damage on a hit. Matching d8s make it leap to another target.
  • CommandSpeak a one-word order (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel or Halt). On a failed Wisdom save, the creature follows it on its next turn. Slots above 1st add a target.
  • Dragon's BreathTouch a willing creature and pick Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning or Poison. It can exhale a 15-foot Cone for 3d6 damage (Dexterity save for half) as a Magic action.
  • Fearat 5Creatures in a 30-foot cone make a Wisdom save or drop what they hold and become Frightened. They run from you by the safest route, and can retry the save when they lose sight of you.
  • Flyat 5Touch a willing creature to give it a 60-foot Fly Speed and the ability to hover for up to 10 minutes. It falls when the spell ends. Higher slots add one target per level above 3.

Wild Magic p. 149. Your spells sometimes set off random surges, and now and then you can give yourself advantage on a roll. There's no free spell list with this one.

Warlock PHB p. 153

A warlock only gets two spell slots, but they're always at full strength and they come back after a short rest. If your group takes a lot of breathers, that adds up fast. Warlocks are also a little tougher, with more hit points and light armor.

  • Eldritch Invocations (level 1, p. 153). Permanent magic secrets. You have 5 by level 5. The pacts people talk about (a summoned weapon, a familiar, a book of extra magic) are picked from these.
  • Pact Magic (level 1, pp. 153–154). Your two slots are level 3 now and refill on a short rest.
  • Magical Cunning (level 2, p. 154). A minute-long ritual that gets one slot back, once a day.

Patrons, chosen at level 3

Archfey p. 159. A fey noble. You can blink a short distance for free a few times a day, and each blink comes with a bonus, like a few temporary hit points.

Free spells:

  • Calm EmotionsHumanoids in a 20-foot radius make Charisma saves. Affected ones either become immune to Charmed and Frightened or turn Indifferent to chosen foes.
  • Faerie FireLight up objects and creatures in a 20-foot cube; a Dexterity save decides who gets outlined. Outlined targets can't turn Invisible, and attacks against them have Advantage.
  • Misty StepAs a Bonus Action, teleport up to 30 feet to an open space you can see.
  • Phantasmal ForcePlant an illusion in one creature's mind (up to a 10-foot cube) if it fails an Intelligence save. It treats the illusion as real until it succeeds at an Investigation check against your save DC.
  • SleepCreatures in a 5-foot-radius area within 60 feet make a Wisdom save or become Incapacitated, then fall Unconscious if they fail a second save. Damage or a shake wakes them.
  • Blinkat 5At the end of each of your turns, roll a d6. On a 4 to 6 you slip into the Ethereal Plane until the start of your next turn, then return nearby.
  • Plant Growthat 5Cast quickly to make plants in a 100-foot radius so thick that moving through costs 4 feet per foot. Cast over 8 hours to enrich plants in a half-mile radius so they yield double.

Celestial p. 160. A heavenly patron. You carry a pool of healing dice (6d6 at level 5) to hand out as a bonus action.

Free spells:

  • AidUp to three creatures within 30 feet gain 5 extra Hit Points, both current and maximum, for 8 hours. Slots above 2nd add 5 more per level.
  • Cure WoundsTouch a creature to restore 2d8 plus your spellcasting modifier in Hit Points. Slots above 1st add 2d8.
  • Guiding BoltHurl a bolt of light at a creature within 120 feet: ranged spell attack for 4d6 Radiant damage. The next attack against the target before your next turn has Advantage. +1d6 per slot level above 1.
  • Lesser RestorationTouch a creature and, as a Bonus Action, end one condition on it: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned.
  • LightTouch an object up to Large size to make it glow like a torch (bright light in 20 feet, dim for 20 more) for an hour. You can choose the color. Covering it hides the glow.
  • Sacred FlameRadiance falls on a creature you can see within 60 feet. Dexterity save or take 1d8 Radiant damage, and cover doesn't help. Damage grows at levels 5, 11 and 17.
  • Daylightat 5A 60-foot-radius Sphere of sunlight appears at a point in range, with Bright Light inside and Dim Light for 60 more feet. It dispels weaker magical Darkness.
  • Revivifyat 5Touch a creature that died within the last minute and it returns to life with 1 Hit Point. Doesn't work on old age or regrow missing body parts. Costs a 300 GP diamond.

Fiend p. 161. A demon or devil. Every time an enemy drops near you, you get temporary hit points. Lots of fire.

Free spells:

  • Burning HandsA sheet of flame fills a 15-foot Cone: Dexterity save or 3d6 Fire damage, half on a success. Ignites loose flammable objects. Slots above 1st add 1d6.
  • CommandSpeak a one-word order (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel or Halt). On a failed Wisdom save, the creature follows it on its next turn. Slots above 1st add a target.
  • Scorching RayHurl three fire rays at one or more targets within 120 feet. Each needs its own ranged spell attack and deals 2d6 Fire damage. Higher slots add one ray per level.
  • SuggestionSuggest a short course of action (25 words or less) to a creature that understands you. On a failed Wisdom save it is Charmed and follows it, until you or your allies hurt it.
  • Fireballat 5A fiery blast explodes at a point within 150 feet. Everything in a 20-foot radius makes a Dexterity save, taking 8d6 Fire damage or half on a success. Adds 1d6 per slot level above 3.
  • Stinking Cloudat 5A 20-foot-radius cloud of foul gas Heavily Obscures the area within 90 feet. Creatures starting their turn inside must pass a Constitution save or lose their action and Bonus Action that turn.

Great Old One pp. 162–163. Something huge and ancient. Mind-to-mind talking at long range, psychic damage on demand, and you can cast charms and illusions without a word or a gesture.

Free spells:

  • Detect ThoughtsSense nearby minds within 30 feet, or read the surface thoughts of a creature you can see. Deeper probing makes the target roll a Wisdom save.
  • Dissonant WhispersOne creature within range hears a hideous melody: Wisdom save or take 3d6 Psychic damage and use its Reaction to run away from you. Half damage on a save.
  • Phantasmal ForcePlant an illusion in one creature's mind (up to a 10-foot cube) if it fails an Intelligence save. It treats the illusion as real until it succeeds at an Investigation check against your save DC.
  • Tasha's Hideous LaughterA creature within 30 feet makes a Wisdom save or falls Prone and Incapacitated, laughing helplessly. It saves again each turn and when hurt (with Advantage on damage). Higher slots add targets.
  • Clairvoyanceat 5Create an invisible sensor up to a mile away, in a place you know or an obvious spot. Choose seeing or hearing through it, and switch with a Bonus Action.
  • Hunger of Hadarat 5Open a 20-foot-radius sphere of Darkness that's Difficult Terrain and blinds anyone inside. Creatures take 2d6 Cold damage starting their turn there, or 2d6 Acid (Dexterity save) ending it there.

Wizard PHB p. 165

The wizard has the most spells by far, all written in a book, and gets to change which ones are ready every morning. Good if you like planning ahead and collecting things.

  • The spellbook (level 1, pp. 165–167). It has 14 spells by level 5, and you can copy in spells you find on adventures (2 hours and 50 gp per spell level).
  • Ritual Adept (level 1, p. 166). Ritual spells can be cast straight from the book without preparing them.
  • Arcane Recovery (level 1, p. 166). Once a day on a short rest, get back one level 3 slot, or a level 2 and a level 1.
  • Scholar (level 2, p. 166). Expertise in one bookish skill, like Arcana or History.
  • Memorize Spell (level 5, p. 167). On a short rest, swap one ready spell for another from the book.

Schools, chosen at level 3

Schools don't come with free spell lists. Each one adds 2 free spells of its school to your book when you pick it, and one more each time a new spell level opens up.

Abjurer p. 172. A magic ward that takes hits for you. Casting protective spells tops it back up.

Diviner p. 173. Every morning you roll two d20s and keep the results. Later you can swap one in for any roll you can see, yours or anyone else's.

Evoker p. 174. Your damage cantrips still do half damage when they miss.

Illusionist p. 175. Silent illusions with extra range, and a free Minor Illusion you can cast as a bonus action.

  • Minor IllusionCreate a sound or an image of an object no bigger than a 5-foot cube for 1 minute. The image has no sound or light, and touching it shows it's fake.

Can't decide? If you want to show off with a few spells, sorcerer. If you want toughness and a patron with a story, warlock. If you want every spell in the world, wizard. And tell your DM everything. The stars already know.
