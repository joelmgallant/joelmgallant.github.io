Agatha

Dwarf, Life Domain cleric, level 5. Rules from the 2024 Player's Handbook.

Four levels at once. Fine. Pull up a stool and we'll go through it.

What you can do on your turn now

You have 2 uses of Channel Divinity. A short rest gives one back and a long rest gives both back. Each use can be any of the first three below. PHB p. 70

Preserve Life action, 1 Channel Divinity
Hold up your holy symbol and hand out 25 hit points (5 times your cleric level) however you like among Bloodied creatures within 30 feet of you, yourself included. Bloodied means down to half their hit points or less. It can't heal anyone past half their maximum, so it's for pulling people out of trouble, not topping them up. p. 73
Divine Spark action, 1 Channel Divinity
Point your holy symbol at one creature you can see within 30 feet. Roll 1d8 and add your Wisdom modifier. Either heal them that much, or make them roll a Constitution save and take that much necrotic or radiant damage (half if they pass). With +3 Wisdom it's 1d8+3 either way. p. 70
Turn Undead, with Sear Undead action, 1 Channel Divinity
Every undead you choose within 30 feet makes a Wisdom save. The ones that fail are frightened and can't act for a minute, and they spend their turns getting away from you. Hitting one ends it for that one. New at level 5, you also roll a d8 for each point of your Wisdom modifier (at least one) and every undead that failed takes that much radiant damage. That damage doesn't break the effect. +3 Wisdom means 3d8. pp. 70–71
Healing spells hit harder
With Disciple of Life, whenever a spell you cast with a slot heals someone, they get an extra 2 plus the slot's level on top. Cure Wounds from a level 1 slot heals 3 extra. Mass Healing Word from a level 3 slot gives 5 extra to each of the up to six people it heals. Cantrips don't use a slot, so they don't count. p. 73
Level 3 spells
You now have two level 3 slots. Revivify is always ready. Touch someone who died in the last minute and they're back at 1 hit point. It eats a diamond worth 300 gp or more, so you need one in your pack before it matters. pp. 73, 312

The big pot of stew is Preserve Life. Don't waste it on one scraped knee.

How cleric spells work

You don't have a spellbook. After every long rest you can change your prepared list, picking any cleric spells you have slots for, up to 9. Swap the lot if the day ahead looks different. p. 69

Wisdom powers all of it. A holy symbol works as your focus, so if your pilgrim's sun badge is your holy symbol, that's what you hold up. p. 69

Casting a level 1+ spell uses a slot of that level or higher. Plenty of spells do more from a higher slot, so Cure Wounds from a level 2 slot heals more than from a level 1 slot (and Disciple of Life adds 4 instead of 3). Cantrips are free, cast them all day.

Concentration spells (marked conc below) last while you keep your mind on them. You can only hold one at a time, and taking damage can break it. Ritual spells can be cast with no slot if you take 10 extra minutes, as long as you have them prepared. p. 235

The other domains

Just so you know what the other clerics are up to. pp. 74–77

Light burns things, with a radiant flash and a reaction that makes attacks miss. Trickery makes an illusion of you to cast spells from and sneak around with. War gets extra weapon attacks as a bonus action and can turn a miss into a hit.

Coming up

At 6 you get Blessed Healer, so healing someone else with a slot heals you too, 2 plus the slot level. Channel Divinity goes up to 3 uses and you get a third level 3 slot. pp. 70, 74

Level 7 brings Blessed Strikes (extra radiant or necrotic damage on a weapon hit, or Wisdom added to cantrip damage, your pick), a level 4 slot, Divine Spark goes to 2d8, and Aura of Life and Death Ward become domain spells. pp. 70–71, 73

  • Aura of LifeA 30-foot aura around you gives you and allies Necrotic resistance, protects Hit Point maximums from reduction, and gives allies at 0 HP 1 Hit Point on their turn.
  • Death WardTouch a creature. The first time it would drop to 0 Hit Points it goes to 1 instead, or it escapes an instant-death effect, and the spell ends. Lasts 8 hours.
