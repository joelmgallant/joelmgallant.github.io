The working theory

My #1 artist by track count is The Menzingers (112). Rancid, NOFX and Rise Against are in the top 30. So how did folk get in?

Mostly through punk singers who picked up an acoustic guitar. Frank Turner (Million Dead), Brian Fallon (The Gaslight Anthem), Dave Hause (The Loved Ones), Chuck Ragan (Hot Water Music), Brendan Kelly (The Lawrence Arms), Jared Hart (The Scandals), Aaron West (Dan Campbell of The Wonder Years). It's the same guys with fewer distortion pedals. The music app filed all of them under "Alternative" and moved on.
